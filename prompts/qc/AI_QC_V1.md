# Miko AI QC V1

Evaluate a generated Miko asset against:
- Master Bible
- Visual Style Bible
- Story Rules
- QC Checklist

Return structured JSON.

## Important
AI QC is advisory. Human QC is the final authority.

Check:
1. Story
2. Character identity
3. Visual consistency
4. Animation
5. Voice
6. Music/SFX
7. Subtitle
8. Technical quality

For every failure, identify the exact scene/asset and provide a concise regeneration instruction.
